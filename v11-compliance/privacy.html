隐私说明 / Privacy notice

范围与联系 / Scope and contact

本机保存 / Local storage

无需注册。闹钟时间、名称、日程、声音、主题、偏好、活动任务与答题进度、起床复查、结果及诊断事件保存在当前浏览器或Android应用沙盒。历史上限为90条结果、300条发生记录、300条事件及100条复查，较旧记录会被移出。

No registration is required. Alarm times, labels, schedules, sounds, themes, preferences, active tasks and answer progress, checks, results and diagnostic events remain in this browser or Android sandbox. History is limited to 90 results, 300 occurrences, 300 events and 100 checks, with older history removed.

网络和托管 / Network and hosting

题包与声音从同一站点加载，活动任务使用本地内容。应用没有上传闹钟名称、答案或结果的接口。公开网站由GitHub Pages托管，GitHub会按其隐私声明处理IP地址和请求等访问数据。Android主要加载随包资源。当前没有广告、分析或支付SDK，也没有应用账号服务。

Content and sounds load from this site and active challenges use local content. No endpoint uploads alarm labels, answers or results. GitHub Pages hosts the website and processes IP addresses and request data under its privacy statement. Android primarily loads bundled resources. There is currently no advertising, analytics or payment SDK or app account service.

权限 / Permissions

网页通知可选并可在浏览器设置撤回。网页需保持可运行，不能保证后台或锁屏提醒。Android精确闹钟、通知和全屏提醒分别受系统设置管理，非必要权限拒绝后仍可访问隐私说明和基础设置。

Browser notifications are optional and revocable in browser settings. The page must remain available and cannot guarantee background or locked delivery. Android precise alarms, notifications and full-screen alerts use separate system settings. Privacy pages and basic settings remain available when nonessential access is refused.

删除和诊断 / Deletion and diagnostics

清除本机数据会在确认后删除此合规版本的本地数据与题库缓存，并取消该版本的Android原生计划。此前版本使用独立存储。网页资源缓存可通过浏览器站点设置清理，Android可清除应用数据或卸载。诊断仅在主动选择时导出，默认省略名称和答案，分享前请检查文件。

After confirmation, Clear local data removes this compliance edition’s local app data and question cache and cancels its Android schedules. Earlier editions have separate storage. Browser resource caches can be cleared in site settings. Android data can be cleared or the app uninstalled. Diagnostics export only when you choose and omit labels and answers by default. Review before sharing.

No purchase, restore, subscription management or ad service is connected. No real access, charge or personalized or non-personalized ad request is created. Connected services require accurate provider, purpose, retention, rights and consent disclosures. Basic alarms and stopping never depend on personalized-ad consent.

音乐与记录 / Music and records

The 12 tracks use developer-provided AI-generated material for in-app preview and alarm use. The app offers no music-file export or secondary commercial license to listeners. Completion and your own confirmation are separate records and neither assesses health nor proves you left bed.

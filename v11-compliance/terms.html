使用条款 / Terms of use

可用性 / Availability

网页提醒需保持页面可运行，后台和锁屏调度受浏览器与系统限制。Android测试包仍需完成真机、厂商待机、重启、蓝牙及电池策略验证。重要事项请同时使用已验证的系统闹钟。

Browser reminders require the page to remain available. Browser and system restrictions affect background and lock-screen delivery. The Android test build still needs physical-device, OEM standby, reboot, Bluetooth and battery testing. For important events, also use a verified system alarm.

免费与会员 / Free features and membership

基础闹钟、停止、6首免费音乐与两套官方主题免费。会员拟在订阅有效期内提供高级音乐、主题和题库访问，以及普通浏览页面去广告。本版周期、当地价格和商店商品未配置，无法购买、恢复、管理订阅、开通试用或扣费。正式商店需展示期限、价格、自动续订、试用及取消条款。

Basic alarms, stopping, 6 free sounds and both official themes are free. Planned membership gives access to premium sounds, themes and collections and removes ordinary-page ads while subscribed. Period, local prices and store products are not configured. This edition cannot purchase, restore, manage a subscription, start a trial or charge you. The connected store must disclose period, price, renewal, trial and cancellation terms.

立即停止 / Immediate stopping

任务和复查页面可进入Emergency，确认后立即停止本次闹钟并取消本次复查，无等待、次数限制、收费或广告条件。主动停止不计为完成，答题与确认不验证是否离床或提供健康结论。

Choose Emergency in a challenge or check and confirm to stop immediately and cancel its follow-up. There is no waiting, use limit, fee or ad condition. Stopping does not mark completion. Answers and confirmations do not verify leaving bed or provide health conclusions.

Music is for in-app preview and alarm use. No music-file export or secondary commercial license is offered to listeners. Do not circumvent access controls. Questions support light morning interaction. Independent human bilingual editorial review is pending, so they do not provide professional or educational certification.
